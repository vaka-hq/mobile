Project Hail Mary is a 2021 science-fiction novel by Andy Weir. It is his third novel, after 2011’s The Martian, and 2017’s Artemis. Set in the near future, it is about the astronaut Ryland Grace, who wakes up from a coma, afflicted with amnesia. He gradually remembers that he was sent to the Tau Ceti solar system, 12 light-years from Earth, to find the cause of a solar dimming event that could cause the extinction of humanity.
